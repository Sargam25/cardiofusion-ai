No symptoms,\n   medications, comorbidities, or vital signs are included.\n\n4. **UCI Cleveland is very small (n=303).**\n   Performance estimates have wide confidence intervals. Results should not be over-interpreted.\n\n5. **PTB-XL collection period (1989–1996).**\n   ECG devices and recording protocols differ from modern clinical practice. Generalizability\n   to contemporary patients is uncertain.\n\n## Model Limitations\n\n6. **Single-label classification for PTB-XL.**\n   PTB-XL records may have co-occurring pathologies.
